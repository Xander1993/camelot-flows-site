// DELIVERABLES

What the engagement covers.

psychology

Catalogue and supplier map

Connect approved catalogues, supplier price lists, product codes, units, minimum order quantities, lead times, and document sources.

smart_toy

Quotation preparation

Structure customer requests, match exact codes, present alternatives separately, and flag stale prices, uncertain stock, missing quantities, or conflicting sources.

monitoring

Commercial control

Keep margin, customer-specific terms, freight, currency, availability, and delivery commitments under authorised human approval.

// PROCESS

A distributor workflow in four stages.

Week 0
Capture the RFQ

Preserve the customer message, files, requested codes, quantities, delivery location, and deadline.

Week 1
Check product and supplier sources

Compare approved catalogues, price lists, stock, MOQ, supplier lead times, alternatives, and product documents.

Week 2+
Review commercial exceptions

A person resolves ambiguous codes, substitutions, stale prices, margin, currency, freight, and delivery conditions.

Ongoing
Issue and synchronise

Generate the approved offer, update CRM or ERP, store the source trail, and create follow-up tasks.

// OUTCOMES

Operational detail and boundaries.

Large catalogues
Use codes and approved attributes to narrow matches; never let similarity silently become a product decision.
Several suppliers
Show source, date, currency, stock confidence, MOQ, and lead time so the reviewer can choose.
Human approval
Alternatives, margin, availability, and commitments remain explicit approval points.
