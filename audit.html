    The defects that lose enquiries silently

    Click-to-call links

    Missing or broken tel: links are the most expensive silent defect for a service business — a real client of mine had six of them, all dead, on the day I checked.

    Business schema

    The LocalBusiness markup Google Maps, local results and AI assistants read. Plus a check for self-serving star markup that risks a Google penalty.

    Contact paths

    Forms, email links, phone, contact page — can a ready-to-buy visitor actually reach you, and how many taps does it take?

    It will not promise you traffic, rankings or a revenue number — nobody can promise those honestly from a single page scan. It finds defects: things that are measurably broken or missing on the page you submit, each one explained in plain language. What you do with the list — fix it yourself, hand it to your developer, or talk to me — is entirely your call.
