A careful WooCommerce migration, stabilized after launch.

Ambienti is a live WordPress and WooCommerce project. The work moved an approved site structure into production while preserving the commerce data and operational settings that already belonged to the live store.

WooCommerce Migration Data preservation Post-launch repair

The constraint

Change the experience without replacing the business data.

The production store already contained orders, customers, prices, reviews, product data, and configured payment and shipping behavior. Those records had to remain authoritative during the launch.

The method

Backups, explicit gates, and a reversible replay.

The approved structure was replayed into production with backups and rollback points around each mutation. The live commerce configuration was checked again after launch instead of being assumed from the staging copy.

Production stabilization

The work continued through the shared WooCommerce patterns.

Catalog normalization

Duplicate size terms were normalized while keeping variation relationships, product metadata, and WooCommerce lookup records aligned.

Media repair

A homepage image was restored as a proper WordPress media-library attachment so editing and responsive rendering continued to work normally.

Rendered verification

A duplicated price label and related storefront output were checked against the live rendered pages after the underlying records and templates were repaired.
