## Shared Constraints

- Landscape web asset.
- Cozy solo freelancer web developer and AI automation specialist.
- Warm parchment, terracotta, sage, muted cobalt, charcoal, and natural wood palette.
- No readable text, no brand logos, no watermark, no fake UI words.
