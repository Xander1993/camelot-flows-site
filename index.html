// STRAIGHT_ANSWERS

Straight answers

Practical boundaries before a pilot begins.

Does AI approve prices or commitments? expand_more

No. The workflow can prepare, compare, flag, and route information, but an authorised person approves pricing, margin, delivery conditions, exceptions, and commitments.

Do I need to replace my ERP or CRM? expand_more

Usually no. The first pilot works around the tools and sources already used, with explicit permissions and failure handling.

Will my business show up on Google? expand_more

I set the foundation so you can be found: your Google Business Profile is optimized, the site has on-page local SEO targeting the searches that actually bring paying customers, and the LocalBusiness schema Google needs to trust and place you is built in. I can't guarantee a specific ranking position — nobody honest can — but I make sure everything within a builder's control is done right so you're set up to be found.

What does it cost, and are there hidden fees or monthly charges? expand_more

One fixed price, agreed upfront. The Start one-pager begins at €390; the full Found build is €690–€1,200, fixed to your page count. No surprises and no required monthly fee — you own the asset when it's done. There's an optional Hosting & Care plan if you want me to keep handling hosting, SSL, backups, and updates afterward, but it's opt-in and sold as technical work performed, never as a traffic or ranking promise.

Do you work with businesses outside Moldova? expand_more

Yes. I'm a solo atelier based in Chișinău working with local service businesses across Moldova and Romania first, and the EU and US next. Everything is delivered remotely with direct builder access — no agency layers, no account managers, no middlemen between you and the person actually building your site.

What kind of businesses is this built for? expand_more

Local service businesses that already have demand — salons, clinics, dentists, restaurants, trades, fitness studios, and similar — who are losing customers to a weak, outdated, or unfindable website. If you have a reputation and repeat customers but your site doesn't reflect it, that's exactly what this is for. If you have no demand yet and need marketing to create it from scratch, this isn't the right offer.
