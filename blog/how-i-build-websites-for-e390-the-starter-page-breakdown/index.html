A €390 website sounds like a joke or a trap. Here is the truth: it is a real, functional, client-ready web presence. And in 2025, it comes with AI built in.

Two Packages, One Price

Every €390 project is one of two things:

  • Landing Page + Smart Chatbot – A single, conversion-focused page that captures leads around the clock, with a basic AI chatbot that qualifies visitors, answers FAQs, and routes serious inquiries to your inbox.
  • Portfolio Site (5-10 pages) + Chatbot + Auto-Blog – A full multi-page WordPress site with your services, about, portfolio, and contact pages – plus the same smart chatbot, plus an auto-blog system that generates relevant content and keeps your site fresh without you writing a word.

Both options are fixed-scope. You pick the one that fits your situation.

What a Smart Chatbot Actually Means

This is not a FAQ widget. The chatbot is powered by a basic n8n workflow connected to Claude AI. It reads your service list, understands your pricing, and has natural conversations with potential clients – 24 hours a day, in whatever language they type in. When someone is ready to hire, it captures their details and sends them straight to your email or CRM.

No live chat agent required. No missed leads at 2am.

What Auto-Blog Means

The auto-blog feature (available on the portfolio package) connects to an n8n workflow that generates and publishes relevant blog posts on a schedule. It is not spam – it is keyword-targeted, niche-specific content that builds your SEO footprint while you sleep. You can review and approve each post, or set it to fully automatic. Your call.

Why the Economics Work

The site itself is built on a refined WordPress template – clean, fast, mobile-perfect. I have built this template dozens of times so setup time is minimal. That is what keeps the price low.

The AI layer (chatbot + auto-blog) runs on tools I have already configured for my own agency workflow. Spinning up an instance for your project costs me almost nothing extra. What you are paying for is my time to customize it to your brand, write your copy, configure the automation, and launch. That is it.

Who Is This For

  • Freelancers and consultants who need a professional web presence fast
  • Local businesses with zero web presence right now
  • Service providers who want leads coming in while they work
  • Anyone who has been quoted €2,000–€5,000 for a website and wants a smarter, leaner entry point

Poti revizui si aproba fiecare articol sau il setezi pe pilot automat complet. Alegerea ta.
