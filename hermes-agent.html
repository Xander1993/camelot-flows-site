Not every enquiry is worth your time.

Hermes reads incoming leads against your qualifying criteria — budget, timeline, fit — and scores them before you ever open the thread. High-value leads get escalated instantly. The rest get a polite nurture sequence.

Your inbox, sorted before you get to it.

Hermes reads every email, categorises it (client, lead, vendor, spam), drafts a response for your review, and routes urgent threads to the top. You arrive at a managed inbox, not a firehose.

Every contact, every note, zero manual entry.

Form fills, email threads, call notes — Hermes pushes them all to your CRM in real time. No copy-paste, no missed follow-ups. Your pipeline reflects reality, not last-week's memory.

Common questions answered before you wake up.

Hermes handles the 80% of support queries that don't need you: pricing, availability, process, how-tos. Complex edge cases are flagged and handed off. Your customers get an instant reply. You keep your focus.
