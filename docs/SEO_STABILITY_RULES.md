# SEO stability rules

- Do not redesign the entire site again for at least 90 days after production release.
- Do not rename core URLs without a documented migration reason and one-to-one redirect.
- Do not change the primary positioning every few weeks.
- Do not request indexing after every small edit.
- Do not publish large volumes of generic AI content.
- Do not create pages without clear search intent and internal links.
- Keep the old blog redirects active.
- Monitor Search Console weekly, not obsessively every day.
- Judge progress by non-brand impressions, queries, landing pages, and qualified contacts — not by `site:` results.
- Publish a maximum of one or two strong topical resources per month initially.
- Update existing pages when real client evidence becomes available.
- Keep one primary language per indexable URL and validate reciprocal hreflang after every locale change.
- Keep prices, delivery commitments, contracts, exceptions, and safety-critical decisions under explicit human approval in product copy and demonstrations.
