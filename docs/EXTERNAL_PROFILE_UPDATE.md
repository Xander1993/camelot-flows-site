## Long description

Camelot Flows is the independent workshop of Moldova-based specialist Alexandru Buzi. I help technical equipment distributors, importers, HVAC and refrigeration companies, installers, service firms, and small manufacturers connect customer requests, supplier information, Excel or ERP, commercial documents, and CRM into one supervised workflow. Automation prepares repetitive work and surfaces exceptions; authorized people retain final control over prices, delivery commitments, contracts, and technical or safety-critical choices. I also build B2B websites, WooCommerce stores, multilingual sites, client portals, and integrations when a company needs a stronger digital entry point. Work begins with a bounded workflow diagnostic and fixed-scope pilot. The client retains ownership of its systems and data.
